Welcome to Witch Tower

Witch Tower is a boardgame library and community hub where players can browse available games, check upcoming events, and plan their next game night in advance.

What you can do here

  • Browse the game library and see what is available
  • Check player counts, age guidance, and game details
  • Read and leave reviews as a registered member
  • View upcoming events and planned sessions
  • Send a message through the Contact page if you have a question

Why this page exists

The goal of Witch Tower is to help players decide what to play before they meet, instead of spending the first part of the evening choosing a game. It also helps keep track of the library, available titles, and community plans.

For members

Signed-in users can leave reviews and use the contact form to ask about borrowing games, upcoming or current events, or general questions.
